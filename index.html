Addition by Subtraction

Psychologists often call this concept "addition by subtraction." Eliminating a major behavioral bottleneck, like doomscrolling for two hours, drinking too much, or chronic procrastination, frees up so much baseline time, energy, and dopamine that your life improves drastically without you even trying to start a "new" positive habit.

Most trackers add habits. MinusOne removes the one behavior costing you the most, one boss battle at a time.
